03 / From discovery to decision

A Litbuy shopping guide built around better questions.

The most useful Litbuy finds are the ones you can evaluate clearly. Keep source information beside your notes so you can distinguish listing claims from details you have checked.

Start your search ↗

Start with a category

Choose shoes, clothing, headwear, or accessories. Save the product URL, item name, seller, and selected variant. Keep similar items together so comparisons stay meaningful.

Check measurements and materials

Compare the listing’s size chart with an item you own. Look for garment measurements, shoe length, head circumference, or bracelet dimensions. Record stated materials and flag missing details.

Estimate the complete cost

Separate the item price from domestic delivery, service charges, international shipping, and potential import costs. Use a consistent currency and mark estimates clearly.

Review before ordering

Reopen the source listing to confirm availability and selected options. Review photos, seller information, and applicable return terms. Check source-link support with your chosen purchasing service.

Quick answers about this Litbuy spreadsheet resource and the checks worth making before a purchase.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

In this guide, a Litbuy spreadsheet means an organized list of product links and comparison notes. It helps readers record categories, sizes, prices, and questions before deciding what to buy.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a browsing guide, category links, and a comparison checklist. It does not host a downloadable spreadsheet or a live inventory feed.

How should I use Litbuy product links?

Open the destination listing, confirm the exact item and variant, and record measurements, materials, prices, and seller information. Check whether your chosen purchasing service supports the source URL before placing an order.

Do product photos prove authenticity or quality?

No. Photos can help compare visible details, but they do not establish authenticity, materials, durability, or seller authorization. Ask the seller for reliable supporting information where needed.

Why can the total cost differ from the listed price?

The displayed item price may exclude domestic delivery, service charges, international shipping, currency conversion, and applicable import costs. Check the current terms for your destination and purchasing method.

Is CAFANS an official Litbuy or brand website?

This is an independent discovery guide. It does not claim endorsement by Litbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci, or Hermès. Category links lead to CAFANS.
